Frozen food portions

Can You Freeze This has good freezer tips.

  • 7 lb meat per person per month is a good amount
  • 2 spicy pork sausage link, skins removed
  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 1/4 pizza dough
  • Ham Steaks - Oven 170 Degrees for 5 min to get rid of some of excess water Then cut in half and freeze
